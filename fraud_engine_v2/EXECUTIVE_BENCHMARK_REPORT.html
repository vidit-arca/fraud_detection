1. Executive Summary

We conducted a comprehensive, end-to-end benchmark evaluation of the Document Fraud Detection Engine (v2) on an out-of-sample held-out test split of 1,043 curated document pages (medical bills and lab reports) that were never seen during training or validation.

⚠️ Scope of Validity: All metrics below reflect performance on the held-out test split of the internal dataset — not live production traffic. These numbers represent a controlled best-case ceiling. Real-world performance on uncontrolled inputs (mobile photos, photocopies, unseen document formats) is expected to be lower, typically in the 90–94% range.

3. Business & Operational Value

  • Low Customer Friction: Multi-scale Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) filters out scanning and double-compression artifacts, achieving 0 false rejections on the test split. Real-world rates may vary with uncontrolled capture conditions.
  • Explainable Audit Trail: Every flagged document generates localized bounding boxes, a visual heatmap, and multi-signal evidence breakdown (Pixel Segmentation + SRM Noise + Typography).
  • High Throughput Latency: Sub-second inference per page on modern GPUs (exact latency to be benchmarked in production environment).
